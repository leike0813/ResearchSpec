## Compositions and control

`research-to-patent` retains research report, synthesis and academic source
grading together with actual technical materials in a case index before the
patent docket. It does not represent a literature synthesis as an invention or
skip patent prior-art analysis.

`patent-informed-paper` begins at a human source-scope Gate, waits for both
academic and patent children, then passes the bridge's real Markdown
bibliography and synthesis to the existing writing child. Academic citations
and grading remain present; patent-derived assertions keep their source type,
locations and evidence limitations. Graph tests cover reachable roots, child
role mappings, blocked joins and the writing input paths.

All eighteen stages are fixed registry members, with seven authored profiles.
Navigate remains the single base host entry. No patent-specific workflow
engine branch, new public command, plugin-domain membership or model-service
integration was added. Existing ARSU package/profile bytes remain intact and
its maintenance check uses its own original scope.
