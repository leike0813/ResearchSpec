# Response to Reviewers

**Editor's decision:** Major revision.

We thank the editor and reviewers for their careful reading. We respond to each comment below, in the order received, and quote each comment in italics. The original claim IDs (`CLM-01`, `CLM-02`, `CLM-03`) are preserved; any strength changes are flagged where they appear in the claims registry.

## Major comments

**R1.**
> The manuscript should state that all evidence is local and synthetic before presenting findings.

**Accepted.** We have added a *Scope and Evidence Boundaries* paragraph immediately before the preliminary findings. It states that the evidence base is the four-item local corpus supplied to the manuscript, that there is no comparison group or exposure assignment, and that the findings are scope-bounded observations and labelled hypotheses rather than generalisable causal claims. The same paragraph is cross-referenced from the new Methods section so readers cannot miss it.

**R2.**
> `CLM-02` is too strong. Revise it to reflect the trade-off between faster feedback and verification work, or remove it.

**Accepted in revised form.** `CLM-02` is retained with its stable ID, but the claim statement is rewritten: *"Instructors using generative AI for formative feedback report shorter turnaround alongside additional time spent verifying unsupported claims; the net effect on instructor workload is not measured by the supplied evidence."* The Preliminary Findings paragraph is aligned to this calibration and the older causal phrasing is dropped. The underlying source (`SYN-INTERVIEW-02`) and its limits (self-reported workload, five-instructor convenience sample, no time log) are unchanged.

**R3.**
> The relationship proposed in `CLM-03` is not directly tested. Treat it as a future research hypothesis.

**Accepted, with the underlying idea preserved.** As flagged in our revision context, we agree that the link between disclosure clarity and reduced student uncertainty is not directly tested by the supplied evidence. We (i) set `CLM-03`'s strength to `hypothesis_only`, (ii) restated it without causal wording — *"Clear disclosure guidance may help reduce student uncertainty about acceptable AI use; the supplied materials do not directly compare policy clarity with uncertainty"* — and (iii) moved the substantive sentence into a clearly labelled *Hypotheses for future research* subsection in the Discussion. The survey (`SYN-SURVEY-03`) and policy excerpt (`SYN-POLICY-04`) are now cited only as the source of the hypothesis, not as evidence of the relationship.

**R4.**
> Add a methods section explaining how the four supplied sources were selected and why causal inference is unavailable.

**Accepted.** A new *Methods and evidence boundaries* section is now placed between Introduction and Findings. It lists the four sources with their supplied scope (one first-year writing course observation summary; five-instructor interview summary; 84-response student survey summary; one institutional policy excerpt), states that they were drawn from the fixed local benchmark package with no additional sampling, and explains why causal inference is unavailable: no comparison group, no measured pre/post writing-quality outcome, and no time log. The section also points to the per-claim limits already recorded in `claims.yaml` and restates that the paper is framed as scope-bounded hypothesis generation rather than a causal study.

## Minor comments

**R5.**
> Use consistent terms for "AI-assisted feedback" and "generative AI feedback".

**Accepted.** *AI-assisted feedback* is now used uniformly in the body, and a one-sentence working definition has been added in §1 (feedback on student writing in which a generative AI tool contributes to drafting or evaluating the response). The reviewer and editor excerpts above preserve the original wording.

**R6.**
> Make the limitations visible in the conclusion, not only in methods.

**Accepted.** The conclusion now opens with a limitations paragraph that mirrors the boundaries stated in Methods (single course / five instructors / 84 voluntary respondents / one policy excerpt; no causal inference available). The substantive paragraph that follows is framed in terms of scope-bounded observations and labelled hypotheses, so a reader who reaches the conclusion first still encounters the same caveats.

## Summary of changes

- New *Scope and Evidence Boundaries* paragraph inserted before preliminary findings.
- New *Methods and evidence boundaries* section added between Introduction and Findings.
- `CLM-02` recalibrated to the trade-off wording; no claim added or removed.
- `CLM-03` reframed as a hypothesis, with causal wording removed; underlying idea kept in the Discussion under *Hypotheses for future research*.
- Terminology standardised on *AI-assisted feedback*; working definition added in §1.
- Limitations paragraph moved from methods-only to the opening of the conclusion.

- `CLM-01` remains tentative: the one positive observation rests on a single course with no comparison group and no validated writing-quality measure.
- This revision introduces no new data, sources, analyses, or completed ethics review.
