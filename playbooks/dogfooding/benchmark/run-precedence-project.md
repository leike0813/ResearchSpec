# Project intent

## Research question

How do the supplied synthetic accounts of generative AI in university writing instruction bear on the claims in `benchmark/partial-manuscript.md`?

## Scope and boundaries

Use `benchmark/sources.yaml` and the partial manuscript to assess claims about revision, feedback, instructor workload, and academic integrity. The current research stage prepares evidence for this same manuscript. These materials are synthetic and local; no external literature has been verified.

## Method stance

Separate source observations, interpretations, and unknowns. Do not infer general or causal effects from short classroom observations, convenience interviews, or voluntary survey summaries.

## Expected contribution

An evidence-bound research report, annotated bibliography, and synthesis report for the later manuscript workflow.
