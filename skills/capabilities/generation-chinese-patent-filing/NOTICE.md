# Chinese Patent Helper sources

Joshua Reed (leike0813) authorized MIT distribution of the original material
from [Chinese Patent Helper](https://github.com/leike0813/chinese_patent_helper)
at revision `14d40f003e9ca7d1d55dabbbd7d1aea455367368`.

The bundled five-book DOCX is the unchanged China National Intellectual
Property Administration template. The official download page publishes the
template for applicants and patent agencies to use with the Word to XML editor.
The template has no declared open-source license. Its identity is recorded
separately under `LicenseRef-CNIPA-Official-Template`.
[CNIPA download page](https://cponline.cnipa.gov.cn/GzfwYwblGlwhTMVC/GzfwYwblGlwhT/selectToolsById?weihuRid=157)

The bundled `sRGB-v4.icc` is a Compact ICC Profiles file released under
CC0 1.0 Universal.
[Source revision](https://github.com/saucecontrol/Compact-ICC-Profiles/blob/8e22c542d632e8800625581c4b417a2d081c02b4/profiles/sRGB-v4.icc)
[CC0 dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
