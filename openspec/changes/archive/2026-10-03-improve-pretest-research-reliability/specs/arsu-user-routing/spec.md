## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Ordinary Work Uses Advisory Material Inspection

Navigate SHALL use material inspection on demand for uncertain inputs, exchanges and recovery. Missing material SHALL suspend only dependent work. Independent work and usable partial deliverables SHALL remain possible, with limitations stated. Neither ordinary continuation nor inspection SHALL require a run, approval certificate or persistent activation record.

#### Scenario: Review comments are missing
- **WHEN** reviewer-specific replies depend on absent comments
- **THEN** Navigate asks one focused question about those comments and may continue unrelated manuscript work without inventing replies

### Requirement: Ordinary Composition Remains Open World

Navigate SHALL allow explicit ordinary material exchange and native Agent transformations when no declared Procedure link exists. It SHALL explain material purpose and evidence limits and SHALL NOT claim satisfaction of an unmet Procedure contract. Formal graph controls SHALL retain their current authority.

#### Scenario: Two standalone Procedures have no declared link
- **WHEN** ordinary work can proceed by interpreting or transforming the first Procedure's output
- **THEN** Navigate may bridge it through native capabilities without constructing graph state or requiring a matching catalog role
