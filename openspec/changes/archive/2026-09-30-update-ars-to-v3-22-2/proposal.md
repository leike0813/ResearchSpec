# Proposal

## Why

ARS v3.22.2 adds output-language declarations, citation repairs, acronym diagnostics and stronger instruction/data boundaries. ResearchSpec still pins v3.21.1; its reviewed payload and maintenance evidence need a coherent increment.

## What Changes

- Pin the latest released ARS source and refresh only affected extraction artifacts.
- Adapt writing, citation, review and recovery obligations to the existing capabilities.
- Regenerate ARSU and capability packages, then establish a new semantic-review audit anchor.
- Keep upstream run-ledger, acronym scripts, provider transports and locale installation outside the distributed runtime.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `arsu-converter`: admit the reviewed release and preserve language, evidence and advisory boundaries.

## Impact

Vendor pin, authoring inputs, converter policy and anchor metadata, generated packages, maintenance records and instructions. Public CLI, core schemas and dependencies retain their existing contracts. The academic-paper profile explicitly binds intake configuration to drafting and abstract nodes so language pair and cardinality reach their consumers.
