## Competitive position research

Select primary peers and emerging threats using explicit criteria such as customer problem, product mix, geography, business model, scale, maturity, and capital structure. Test metric comparability before ranking firms. Compare revenue growth, margins, cash conversion, returns, market share, pricing power, switching costs, network effects, cost advantages, intangible assets, distribution, innovation, and execution.

Use `resources/agent-specs/competitor_analysis.json` for landscape and moat analysis. Use `resources/python/financial_analyzer.py` with an injected financial-metrics provider for multi-year peer tables. The upstream prompt permits judgments about relative valuation, market position, moat durability, investment attractiveness, and whether performance supports the current multiple; retain both supporting and contrary evidence.

Explain peer exclusions, accounting or period mismatches, and where comparisons are only directional. Conclude whether the advantage is widening, stable, or narrowing, why, and what evidence could reverse that conclusion.
